## Important Notes

1. **Both platforms require active trading volume.** Add more liquidity before submitting.
2. **CoinGecko typically lists within 1-2 weeks** after submission if requirements are met.
3. **CMC can take 2-4 weeks** and may request additional information.
4. **Logo Requirements:**
   - CoinGecko: 512x512 PNG, transparent background
   - CMC: 200x200 minimum, PNG format
5. **After adding liquidity**, resubmit if initially rejected for low volume.
