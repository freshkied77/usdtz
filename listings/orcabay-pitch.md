## The Opportunity

USDTZ occupies a unique niche: **a privacy-enabled stablecoin with a full DeFi ecosystem**. While UST, USDD, and others focused solely on peg mechanics, USDTZ integrates zero-knowledge privacy, prediction markets, cross-chain bridging, and fiat on-ramping into a single cohesive protocol.

The privacy stablecoin market is effectively unaddressed — no major BSC stablecoin offers ZK-SNARK private transactions. This gives USDTZ a defensible first-mover position.
